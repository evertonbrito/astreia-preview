The point

Research in orbit.
Progress on Earth.
A complete path between them.

Astreia is developing the systems to take research and materials into microgravity, support work in orbit and bring samples back for analysis on Earth.

How we get there

Three systems,
one operating model.

01 · Orbital platform

Tessera-1

An orbital platform in development for research and manufacturing in microgravity, designed around sealed modules for individual processes.

Explore Tessera-1 →
02 · Return vehicle

Thor XR-1

A return vehicle in development to bring research samples and materials from orbit back to Earth for analysis and further work.

Explore Thor XR-1 →
03 · Mission software

OPERA

Mission software in development to connect planning, monitoring and operations across the orbital platform and return vehicle.

    Prepare the research payload and plan its journey to the orbital platform.

  2. 02 / TransformOrbit · Tessera-1

  3. 03 / Down · Thor XR-1Back on Earth

How we build

Build carefully.
Learn from evidence.

Evidence over assumptions

We design tests to answer specific engineering questions. Results from testing and flight are intended to guide the next iteration.

Constraints guide the design

We design around launch interfaces and payload requirements. Practical constraints shape how the systems are developed.

Test risks in stages

We aim to isolate technical risks and test them in stages, so each result informs what comes next.

Learn from every test

Useful testing includes examining what did not work. Clear results support the next engineering decision.

Built around the mission

The research objective, payload requirements and return plan guide the mission design.
